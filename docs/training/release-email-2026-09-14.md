# MakeReady update — email body (14 September 2026)

**Suggested subject:** MakeReady update — finance is live, real balances loaded, and a full user guide in the app

---

Team,

A fair amount has landed in MakeReady over the past two weeks, and most of it
changes how the day-to-day work gets done. Here is the short version.

**The finance side of the business now runs in MakeReady.**
We went through the SAP system in detail — every report, every process, every
month-end routine — and rebuilt it. That includes the October–September fiscal
calendar with periods that open, close and lock; the segmented general ledger
and a P&L by segment; receivables (credit memos, applying one payment across
several invoices, deposits, statements, aging); collections worklists that tell
the AR desk who to ring today, whose card to run, and what must be collected
before it ships; payables (weekly payment runs, vendor credits, goods received
but not yet invoiced); the payroll journal with its month-end accrual; employee
expense claims reimbursed through Accounts Payable; sales tax by state with a
filing worksheet; sales goals against actuals by rep by month; commission; and
the weekly flash and quarterly pack.

**It is loaded with real numbers, not empty screens.**
The open items came across from SAP: 1,978 open customer invoices worth
$2,050,117.68, 97 unapplied credit memos worth $17,044.11, and 1,095 open
supplier bills worth $703,428.68. The general ledger carries every month from
January 2008 through to the cutover on 23 July 2026, and it agrees with SAP:
total assets of $8,008,756.61 against SAP's $8,008,756.62, liabilities to the
cent, and monthly revenue and costs matching SAP to the cent as well. Old
document numbers still work — a customer quoting an invoice number
will be found, and the same is true for supplier bills. In short, collections,
aging, statements and payables are real from day one.

**Reports now have proper permissions.**
Every report can be controlled individually: who can see it, who can change it,
who can delete it. Built-in reports can be restricted to particular roles or
people, and saved reports can be private, shared, or open to everyone. Two
reports that had stopped working are fixed, and the Sales Analysis report — which
was timing out entirely — now returns in under a second.

**Two things that were quietly annoying are fixed.**
The left-hand menu now opens one section at a time, so the list stays short
enough to read. And you will no longer be signed out while you are working: the
sixty-minute timeout is now measured from when you last *did* something, not
from when you signed in. Tick "remember me" and that stretches to thirty days.

**There is now a user guide inside the platform.**
Help → User Guide walks the whole job from the first enquiry through to a closed
month, and for every step it names the screen you do it on — nineteen articles
with a picture of each screen as it looks today. There is a Word copy to
download from the Help page if you would rather print it or keep it to hand.
Start at "How the job runs, and how to find your way around".

**Also shipped in this stretch:** fixed assets and depreciation, cost centres
and job costing, quality inspections, equipment maintenance, workflows and
approvals, and the Content Library moving its files onto Azure storage.

  usual first.last@g54.com pattern as a placeholder. Please check the addresses
  are right before we activate them and send the invitations — nothing goes out
  until then.
- The Content Library is ready but not yet switched on: it still needs the Azure
  share name and credentials.
- Zoey is being replaced rather than connected to. The catalogue in Zoey is

  still to do before we can turn Zoey off.

Anything that looks wrong — particularly in the numbers — please say so early.
Every change is logged, so it is straightforward to see what happened and when.
