# Quoting in MakeReady — A Guide for Sales Reps

**Moving off the Excel order forms (Caps, Baja, Softgoods, Wood) to the MakeReady Quote Builder**

_Great Mountain West · MakeReady training · July 25, 2026_

---

## The big picture

For years, quoting meant opening the right Excel **"Master" order form** for the product, typing into the white cells, and letting the grey **"Formulas – Do Not Type"** cells fill in the unit price. Every product had its own file, and the price lists lived in tabs like **CAP PRICING**.

**MakeReady replaces all of those spreadsheets with one Quote Builder.** The same formulas now run inside the app: you pick the product and quantity, and the price fills itself in. No separate file per product, no chasing the current price list.

## What changed at a glance

| On the Excel order forms | In MakeReady |
|---|---|
| A different file per product (Caps, Baja, Softgoods, Wood) | One Quote Builder; pick a **template** |
| Grey "Do Not Type" cells calculated the unit price | Unit price **auto-fills** from the quantity band |
| CAP PRICING tab (style × quantity) | **Quantity bands** on the item (72/144/288/432/576) |
| 2XL +$2, 3XL +$3 size cells | **Size dropdown** adds the upcharge automatically |
| Screen prep / art / rush cells, New vs Reorder | **Charges & setup** checkboxes + a **Reorder** toggle |
| "Min 144" notes | **Below-minimum warning** on the line |
| Emailing a spreadsheet, re-keying into an order | One-click **email**, then **convert to an order** |

## Caps — quantity bands (the CAP PRICING tab)

On the caps form, the unit price came from the CAP PRICING tab based on **style** (RC, REN, VEL, Animal) and **quantity band**. In MakeReady:

1. Add a line and pick the cap.
2. Type the **quantity**.
3. The **unit price fills in from the band** and updates as the quantity changes — e.g. **RC**: 72 → $10.50, 144 → $9.25, 288 → $8.25, 432 → $8.00, 576 → $7.75.

You'll see **"auto-priced by quantity band"** under the line. If you're under the style's minimum (e.g. Animal caps' 144), you'll get a **"below minimum"** note.

> The unit price for band items is **read-only** — locked to the band, just like the grey cells you weren't supposed to type in. For a genuine one-off, choose **"custom"** on the line and type your own price.

## Apparel — size upcharges (Baja & Softgoods)

Pick the **Size** from the dropdown on the line and the upcharge is added on top of the base price (e.g. **2XL +$2**, **3XL +$3**) — the same as the size cells on the Baja form.

## Decoration, screens, art & rush (Softgoods)

The screen/art/rush charges are the **"Charges & setup"** checkboxes:

- **New ASI Screen Prep** — $15/color
- **Reorder Screen Prep** — $7.50/color
- **Mid-run color change** — $15/color
- **Art / rework** — $65/hour
- **Rush** — +10% (2 weeks) or +20% (1 week)

Tick the ones that apply; per-color and per-hour charges ask for the count. **Tick "Reorder"** on a repeat job and the new-only screen prep drops off automatically — exactly like choosing New vs Reorder on the paper form.

## Step-by-step

1. **Sales → Quotes → "New quote."**
2. Pick the **customer** and the **product template** (Caps (OSH), Baja Hoodies, Softgoods, Wood Products), then open the builder.
3. **Add a line**, pick the item, enter the **quantity** (and **Size** for apparel) — the unit price fills in automatically.
4. Tick any **Charges & setup**; set **Reorder** if it's a repeat.
5. Add **notes/discount** if needed and **Save**.
6. **Email to customer** in one click, or **convert to an order** when accepted.

## After the quote: order, art & proofing

Once the quote is accepted and converted to an order:

1. **Submit to art.** On the order, click **"Submit to art →."** The catalogue image the customer picked and the production spec go to the art department, and the order moves to the **Art & Proof** stage.
2. **Art does the work.** The art team picks it up on the **Art board** (Queue or Kanban), uploads the proposed artwork, and **sends a proof**.
3. **The customer approves on their tracking link.** The proposed image shows up on the same **/track** link the customer already uses to follow their order. They can **Approve**, **Request changes** (with notes), **Decline**, or **Request a meeting** (which offers your self-serve booking link).
4. **You're notified** of their decision, and it's logged to the customer's history. If they request changes, art revises and sends a new proof.

You don't manage the art tools yourself — just submit to art and watch the order advance. For the catalogue image to travel with the order, make sure the item has an image (your admin sets these in the Template Builder).

## FAQ

**Do I still keep my own spreadsheet?** No. Prices are managed centrally by your admin in the Template Builder, so everyone quotes from the same, current numbers.

**A band price is wrong / needs to change.** Tell your admin — they update it once in the Template Builder and every rep's quotes use the new number immediately.

**A product isn't in the list.** Ask your admin to add the template/item, or use a **custom** line for a one-off.

**Can I still discount?** Yes — set a discount on the quote; the total recalculates.
